Stafin Homes collects information to provide better services to all our users. We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, and email address when you submit property inquiries or contact forms.

We use the information we collect from to provide, maintain, protect, and improve our real estate services. Specifically, we use your data to:

3. Information Sharing

We do not share personal information with companies, organizations, or individuals outside of Stafin Homes except in the following cases:

  • With your consent: We will share personal information with companies, organizations, or individuals outside of Stafin Homes when we have your explicit consent to do so (e.g., passing your details to a property owner).

By using the WhatsApp chat feature on our website, you agree to connect with our business account. Standard data and messaging rates may apply through your cellular provider. Your conversation history is subject to WhatsApp's own end-to-end encryption standards and privacy policies.
